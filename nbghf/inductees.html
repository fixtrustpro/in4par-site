Since 1986

The record, every name in it.

Harold Dunovant founded this Hall because the contributions of Black golfers were going unrecorded. What follows is that record: every class from the inaugural induction in 1986 forward, players, builders, organisations and locations.

Help us finish it. Many of these entries have no photograph and no line of biography. If you knew them, played with them, or hold a picture, we want it. The record only stays a record if somebody keeps adding to it.
