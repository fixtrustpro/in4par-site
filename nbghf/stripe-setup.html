Stripe Setup Checklist

Five things to finish so the Hall can take registration payments online. Roughly twenty minutes in total. Tick them off as you go and the page will remember where you got to.

0 of 5 done

Thank you for doing this. Everything below happens inside the Hall's Stripe account at dashboard.stripe.com. Log in there first.

If you cannot find a screen, use the search box at the very top of Stripe and type the word in bold. It jumps straight there. That works for every step here.

Nothing on this list can lose money or break anything. The worst that happens is you tick something twice.

1

Send one dollar to check it works

Do this one first. It takes a minute and it tells us the account is switched on properly. Until this passes, we cannot email the invitations.

Open the $1 test page

  1. Open that link on your phone or computer.
  2. Enter your email and phone number.
  3. Pay the $1 with a real card. It is a real charge, and Ian will refund it.
  4. You should see a thank-you message saying the system is live.

Then tell Ian it went through. If you get an error instead, send him a photo of the error message. That is the useful bit.

2

Ask Stripe for the charity discount

Stripe charges charities less than normal businesses, but only if you ask. This is the one that saves the Hall the most money, so it is worth doing properly.

  1. In Stripe, click the ? help button, near the top right.
  2. Choose to send a message or contact support.
  3. Paste in the message below and send it.
Hello, We are a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit and would like to apply for Stripe's discounted nonprofit processing rate. Organisation: National Black Golf Hall of Fame, Inc. EIN / Tax ID: 26-2420132 Please let us know what documentation you need. Thank you.

Stripe usually replies by email within a day or two. Forward whatever they send to Ian.

3

Create the member discount code

The invitation letters promise Advisory Committee, LPGA, PGA and Hall of Fame members half off. This creates the code that makes that work. Without it, they have no way to claim it.

Part one, the discount.

  1. Go to Product catalog in the left menu.
  2. Click the Coupons tab along the top.
  3. Click Create coupon.
  4. Choose Percentage discount and type 50.
  5. Where it asks how long it lasts, choose Once.
  6. Name it Member 50 percent. Save it.

Part two, the code people type.

  1. Open the coupon you just made.
  2. Find Promotion codes and click to add one.
  3. Type the code exactly: MEMBER50
  4. Save.

Capital letters, no space. It has to match the letters going out.

4

Switch on Apple Pay

Most people will register from their phone. Apple Pay lets them pay with a fingerprint instead of typing a card number, and far more of them finish.

  1. Click the Settings gear, top right.
  2. Find Payment methods. Or type payment methods in the search box at the top.
  3. Turn on Apple Pay.
  4. Turn on Link while you are there. It remembers a card so returning people pay in one tap.

Card and bank transfer are already working, so this is an improvement rather than a fix. If either one is already on, just tick this off.

5

Set the name on card statements, and lock the account

Two small things on the same trip through Settings.

First, the name people see on their bank statement.

  1. Settings, then Business, then Public details.
  2. Find Statement descriptor.
  3. Set it to NBGHF

If somebody sees a name they do not recognise on their statement, they call the bank and reverse the payment. This prevents that.

Second, two-step login.

  1. Settings, then look for Security or Two-step authentication.
  2. Turn it on. A code to your phone is fine.
  3. Stripe will show you recovery codes. Save them somewhere that is not your phone. Print them if that is easiest.

This account will hold real donations. The recovery codes are how you get back in if you lose your phone.
