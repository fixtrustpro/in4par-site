Celebration 120 · October 1 to 3, 2026

One link for your website

Everything below is optional and nothing here asks for access to your site, your logins, or your hosting. It is one web address you can point a button at.

The short version. People who want to register for Celebration 120, buy a souvenir journal ad, book the hotel, or make a donation all need to end up on one page. If a visitor lands on your site instead, they currently find nothing. Adding a single link fixes that.

The link

in4pargolf.com/nbghf/register

That page handles registration, foursomes, gala seats, souvenir journal ads, sponsorships, the hotel room block, and donations. Payments go directly to the National Black Golf Hall of Fame's own Stripe account, a registered 501(c)(3), Tax ID 26-2420132.

Option one, the easiest thing that works

Add a normal link anywhere on the site. A menu item, a line in a news post, a sentence on the home page. Any of these are fine:

  • Register for Celebration 120
  • Celebration 120, October 1 to 3
  • Clearview 80 and NBGHF 40, register here

pointed at https://in4pargolf.com/nbghf/register

If that is all that ever happens, the job is done. Everything below is just nicer looking.

Option two, a button

Paste this wherever your site accepts HTML. It needs no stylesheet and no plugin.

<a href="https://in4pargolf.com/nbghf/register"
   style="display:inline-block;padding:15px 30px;background:#0B1636;
          color:#ffffff;text-decoration:none;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;
          font-size:16px;font-weight:bold;letter-spacing:.08em;
          text-transform:uppercase;border-radius:3px">
  Register for Celebration 120
</a>

WordPress

Add a Custom HTML block and paste the same code. Or use a normal Buttons block and set the link to the address above, which keeps it in your own theme's styling.

Squarespace, Wix, GoDaddy and similar

Add a Button element and paste the address into the link field. There is no need to use the HTML above.

Questions you might reasonably have

Who runs that page?

Ian Saunders of In4Par, working with John Simpson on Celebration 120. John can vouch for it. The page was built for this event at the Hall's request.

Where does the money go?

Straight to the National Black Golf Hall of Fame's own Stripe account. It does not pass through In4Par or anyone else. The Hall controls that account and its bank details.

Do you need access to our website?

No. Nothing here requires a login, a password, or any change to your hosting. You are adding one outbound link.

What if we would rather host the page ourselves?

That works too and it is genuinely better, because visitors never leave your site. Say the word and the whole page will be handed over as plain HTML, or rebuilt on your domain, whichever suits how your site is built.

What if something needs changing later?

The link never changes, so nothing on your side ever needs touching again. Prices, dates and content on the destination page are updated by us.
