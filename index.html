The Ethos

The industry sells you birdie. We tell you the truth about par.

In4Par started with one honest idea: golf is better when somebody finally tells the truth about it. The gear, the game, the culture, the people who make it fun. No sponsorship whispering in our ear, no hype we don't believe. Just straight talk and a whole lot of love for the game.

It grew into something bigger than a voice. It became a house, a studio that makes its own honest content and arms the golfers who deserve to be seen.
