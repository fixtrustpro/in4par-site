2. How we fill the bus in 17 days

1. Call five warm supporters today

The people who have given before already said yes once. Call each one: "We're going back to the Florida Classic on November 21. Can we count on you for five students at $500?" One afternoon of calls is the fastest money there is.

2. Keep one simple list

Who you asked, how much, pledged, received, and the next step. Students signed up and money received are two different counts, so this list tells us exactly what the tickets still need.

3. Five students for $500

A bigger, easier ask than $100 for businesses, churches, ministries, clubs and families. With your OK, every $500 sponsor gets a thank-you post by name, which costs nothing and means a lot to a local business.

4. A matching gift

Ask one sponsor (a dealership, the credit union, Rotary) to match gifts up to $1,000 through November 15. Once a sponsor commits, "every $100 you give is doubled" moves people who were on the fence. We announce the match only after it is confirmed.

5. Rotary and the Chamber

You're a member of both. Ask for two minutes at the next Rotary Club of Sebring meeting and a spot in the Chamber's Friday email. Ask the Avon Park Rotary too.

6. Church Sundays: November 8 and 15

Send the pastor note to five churches. One announcement and a bulletin line on two Sundays reaches more parents and givers than any post.

7. The three schools

Ask each principal to send the parent message home and to confirm how forms come back. That is the fastest way to fill the open seats.

8. Your 80 golfers

One personal text to each, asking for one student. If 1 in 10 says yes, that's eight students. It also warms them up for May 8.

9. HBCU alumni and the Divine Nine

FAMU and Bethune-Cookman alumni and the fraternity and sorority chapters care about this game more than anyone. Ask each chapter to sponsor five future Rattlers and Wildcats.

10. Post the count, with the date

A count every two or three days shows progress, and progress makes people give. Update it after every school count.

11. Game day builds May 8

Approved photos from the bus and the stands, then a thank-you post tagging every sponsor. That builds the case for the May 8 tournament.

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

Saffold Foundation to take Highlands County students to the Florida Classic

SEBRING, Fla. (November 4, 2026) The Robert C. Saffold Sr. Foundation, Inc. will take up to 50 high school students from Avon Park, Sebring and Lake Placid High Schools to the Florida Classic in Orlando on Saturday, November 21, 2026, as part of its University Experience Tour.

The all-expense-paid trip includes round-trip luxury bus transportation, breakfast, an event T-shirt and a ticket to the game between Florida A&M University and Bethune-Cookman University at Camping World Stadium. 25 students have signed up so far.

Students can pick up a trip packet with the permission form in the front office at Avon Park, Sebring or Lake Placid High School. Parents with questions can call or text Robert Saffold Jr. at 863-812-0845.

The Robert C. Saffold Sr. Foundation was founded in Sebring on November 18, 2022, to help Highlands County high school students further their outlook on higher education and vocational opportunities. Its programs include the University Experience Tour, the Stuff the Bus back-to-school supply drive and the Robert C.
