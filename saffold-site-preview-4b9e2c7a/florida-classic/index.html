2. How we fill the bus in 17 days

1. Ask past sponsors first (this week)

The people on last year's sponsor board already said yes once. Text or call each one: "We're back on November 21. Can we count on you for a row of five?" One afternoon of calls is the fastest money there is.

2. Sponsor a Row, $500 for five students

A bigger, easier ask than $100 for businesses, churches, ministries, clubs and families. Every row sponsor gets a thank-you post by name, which costs nothing and is worth a lot to a local business.

3. A matching gift

Ask one sponsor (a dealership, the credit union, Rotary) to match every gift up to $1,000 through November 15. "Every $100 you give is doubled" moves people who were on the fence. Use the match money to buy the tickets.

4. Rotary and the Chamber

You're a member of both. Ask for two minutes at the next Rotary Club of Sebring meeting and in the Chamber newsletter. Ask the Avon Park Rotary too, since it sponsored before.

5. Church Sundays: November 8 and 15

Send the pastor note to five churches. One announcement and a bulletin line on two Sundays reaches more parents and givers than any post.

6. Your 80 golfers

One personal text to each, asking for one student. If 1 in 10 says yes, that's eight seats. It also warms them up for May 8.

7. HBCU alumni and the Divine Nine

FAMU and Bethune-Cookman alumni and local fraternity and sorority chapters care about this game more than anyone. Ask each chapter to sponsor a row of future Rattlers and Wildcats.

8. Post the count

A seat tracker every two or three days shows progress, and progress makes people give. Update the number after every count.

9. Game day content is next year's fundraiser

Photos from the bus, Fan Fest and the stands, then a thank-you post tagging every sponsor. That is the pitch for the 2027 trip and the May 8 tournament.

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

Saffold Foundation to take 50 Highlands County students to the Florida Classic

SEBRING, Fla. (November 4, 2026) The Robert C. Saffold Sr. Foundation, Inc. will take up to 50 high school students from Avon Park, Sebring and Lake Placid High Schools to the Florida Classic in Orlando on Saturday, November 21, 2026, as part of its University Experience Tour.

The all-expense-paid trip includes round-trip luxury bus transportation, breakfast, an event T-shirt, three hours at the Florida Classic Fan Fest and a ticket to the game between Florida A&M University and Bethune-Cookman University. 25 of the 50 seats are filled.

[DRAFT QUOTE, Robert to approve or rewrite:] "For a lot of these students, this is their first look at college life and an HBCU weekend," said Robert C. Saffold Jr., president of the Foundation. "We want every one of them to come home thinking about where they're going next."

The Foundation is asking local businesses, churches, clubs and families to sponsor the remaining seats. A gift of $100 sponsors one student, and $500 sponsors a row of five. Donations can be made by Zelle to 863-812-0845 or by check to the Robert C. Saffold Sr. Foundation, P.O. Box 982, Avon Park, FL 33826. The Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit.

Students can pick up a trip packet with the permission form in the front office at Avon Park, Sebring or Lake Placid High School. Parents with questions can call or text Robert Saffold Jr. at 863-812-0845.

The Robert C. Saffold Sr. Foundation was founded in Sebring on November 18, 2022, to help Highlands County high school students further their outlook on higher education and vocational opportunities. Its programs include the University Experience Tour, the Stuff the Bus back-to-school supply drive and the Robert C. Saffold Sr. Memorial Charity Golf Tournament, held each spring at Golf Hammock Country Club.
