Why Highlands County businesses sponsor

Sponsors stand in front of the people who live and buy here, at an event the county's golfers come back to every year, under the name of a man most of them were taught or coached by. The Foundation is a 501(c)(3); sponsorships are deductible to the extent the law allows.

Thank you to the partners who show up

The Foundation's work in Highlands County has been carried alongside Lakeshore Mall, the Boys and Girls Club of Highlands County, Sebring High School athletics, the Sebring Chamber of Commerce, the Rotary Club of Sebring and the Highlands County NAACP.
