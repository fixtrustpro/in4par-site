Through the year

  • Backpack drive, August. Sort supplies, fill backpacks, staff the table at the Back-to-School Bash.
  • University Experience, November. Chaperone a bus, help with sign-ups and permission forms.
  • Sponsor outreach, January to April. Introduce the Foundation to a business you know. One call, one visit.
  • T-Time listings. Help confirm event details with organizers across Florida from your kitchen table.

Students

Bright Futures requires community service hours. Every role above comes with a signed log and, for a job well done, a reference letter. Bring your counselor's approval form.

Retirees

AmeriCorps Seniors RSVP of Highlands County, at South Florida State College, places volunteers 55 and over with the Foundation at no cost and covers you with insurance on duty. Call (863) 784-7189 and ask for the Saffold Foundation.
