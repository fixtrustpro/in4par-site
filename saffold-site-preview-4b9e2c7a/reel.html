What it says

  1. Robert C. Saffold Sr. Foundation, Highlands County, Florida.
  2. Robert C. Saffold Sr., 1929 to 2021.
  3. Teacher. Coach. Organizer. E.O. Douglas High School, from 1957.
  4. 1972: he convened the Sebring meeting that founded the Sunshine State Golfers Association.
  5. National Black Golf Hall of Fame, Class of 1996. African American Golfers Hall of Fame, 2014.
  6. 5th Annual Memorial Charity Golf Tournament, May 8, 2027, Golf Hammock, Sebring.
