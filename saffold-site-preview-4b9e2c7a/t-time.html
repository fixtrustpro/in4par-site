Founding partners

Built with the clubs that built the game here

T-Time starts with the Sunshine State Golfers Association, which Robert C. Saffold Sr. helped found in 1972, and its affiliate clubs across the state: 2UP Golf Club of Miami, Broken Tee of Titusville, the Broward Ebony Golf Association, Fairview Golf Club of West Palm Beach, Par 5 Golf Club of Fort Pierce, Tri-City of Orlando and PinSeekers of Central Florida. Fraternity, sorority, alumni, church and foundation tournaments are welcome.
