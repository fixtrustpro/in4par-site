Our story

A teacher who coached, a golfer who organized, a man who drove the bus

Robert C. Saffold Sr. taught art and coached basketball and football for twenty-six years at E.O. Douglas High School and Lake Placid High School. In 1972 he convened the meeting at Harder Hall in Sebring that created the Sunshine State Golfers Association, Florida's statewide association of African American golfers, and he served as its president. He built the Washington Heights Youth Center, revived Juneteenth in Sebring, and leased as many as six buses a year so that local children could see a college campus and the Florida Classic.

He was inducted into the National Black Golf Hall of Fame in 1996 and the African American Golfers Hall of Fame in 2014. His son, Robert Jr., founded this Foundation in 2022 to carry the work forward.
