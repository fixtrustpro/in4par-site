For John Simpson · Prepared October 10, 2026

Clearview and NBGHF Celebrity Golf Outreach Plan

A plan for discussion. Nothing has been sent to anyone.

The biggest opening in years: LeBron James is bringing a PGA TOUR event to Akron in July 2027, about 20 miles from Clearview, and a wave of Black athletes now love the game. This plan puts Clearview and the Hall in front of them, led by John and Renee, with no money asked.

Why now

LeBron James took up golf at 40 and has built around it fast. On August 25, 2026, the PGA TOUR and his foundation announced the first LeBron James Family Foundation Invitational, July 6 and 7, 2027, at Firestone Country Club in Akron. It is run with the PGA TOUR's Pathway to Progression program (the APGA and Black collegiate golfers), and those young professionals will mentor students from his I PROMISE school. Participants and details have not been announced, so the event is still being shaped.

  • The fit: LeBron says his goal is to open doors for inner-city kids to be coached and play golf. That is why Clearview was built and why the Hall exists.
  • The timing: the Invitational falls two months before the Class of 2027 induction in Atlanta, when Clearview is scheduled to be inducted.
  • The wave: Steph Curry, J.R. Smith, Kevin Hart and others are publicly invested in golf right now, several of them in Black golf and HBCU golf.

How every letter works

Every letter comes from Renee Powell and John Simpson, makes one specific invitation, and asks only for a short call.

  • One hook: the single fact that ties that person to Clearview (Curry's Akron birth and Sifford Award, J.R.'s HBCU golf, Fitzgerald's Canton enshrinement).
  • One invitation: a round with Renee, a junior golf day, the Atlanta induction, or their media telling Clearview's story.
  • The same history paragraph in every letter, using the Clearview wording John approved for the Class of 2027 page.
  • No money ask in a first letter, and no claim we cannot source.
  • Written to the gatekeeper: the program lead, representative or foundation officer who decides whether the star ever sees it.

Drafts for the event and the first five people are ready for review: LeBron's Invitational, J.R. Smith, Steph Curry, Larry Fitzgerald, Kevin Hart and Charles Barkley. Each one is edited before it reaches John, and nothing goes out without John's and Renee's approval.

Sequence

The event goes first because its plans are still open; each person follows only after the one before has been answered or given time.

  1. October 2026: John sends the short routing note to the PGA TOUR and confirms Renee's involvement.
  2. November 2026: the Invitational letter goes to the event lead. The J.R. Smith and Steph Curry letters follow.
  3. Winter 2027: Larry Fitzgerald, Kevin Hart and Charles Barkley, adjusted to what the first replies teach us.
  4. Spring 2027: visits and filming at Clearview, once the course is in season.
  5. July 6-7, 2027: the Invitational in Akron, with Clearview and the Hall present if the event team agrees.
  6. September 17-18, 2027: the Class of 2027 induction in Atlanta, with invited guests and any recorded messages.

If J.R. Smith responds, he is the most natural person to bring Clearview's story to LeBron personally.
